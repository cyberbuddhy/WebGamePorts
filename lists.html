Bring your own ROMs unless noted. Tech behind most hubs: EmulatorJS (libretro cores → WASM).

🎮 GameCube games online

  • Game libraries (click a game, it boots)
    KhoGameHub GC (Melee, Sunshine, Double Dash, Wind Waker… Play now + netplay/PWA) · Vizzed GameCube (plugin) · playretrogames.online GC (Melee 151k plays, Sunshine, Double Dash)
  • Real GameCube→browser ports (no emu needed)
    Animal Crossing Online (full game, BYO disc, cloud saves) · Monkey Ball Online (SMB1/2 + widescreen)
  • Bring-your-own-ISO frontends
    RetroArch Dolphin core · Netcade · Gecko compat DB (what actually boots)

No general GameCube-in-browser emu exists (WASM lacks JIT/SIMD) — the libraries above stream or use per-game builds; the two ports are native.
